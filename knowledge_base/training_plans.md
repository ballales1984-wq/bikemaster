# Training Plans

## Polarized Training
- **80/20 Principle**: 80% low intensity (Z1-Z2), 20% high intensity (Z4-Z5)
- **Science Backing**: Studies show polarized training produces best results for road cyclists
- **Avoid Gray Zone**: Zone 3 (tempo) training should be minimized - too hard for base, not hard enough for threshold
- **How to Measure**: Use heart rate or power data to verify true time distribution across zones
- **Duration**: Minimum 8-12 weeks to see measurable improvement with polarized approach

## Periodization

### Macrocycle (Annual Plan)
- **Prep Phase** (4-6 weeks): Low volume, focus on general fitness, strength training, technique work
- **Base Phase** (8-12 weeks): High volume Zone 2, build aerobic engine, add strength sessions
- **Build Phase** (6-8 weeks): Increase intensity with VO2 max and threshold intervals, reduce volume slightly
- **Peak Phase** (2-4 weeks): Race-specific intensity, reduce volume, maintain fitness sharpness
- **Recovery Phase** (1-2 weeks): Active recovery only, very low intensity, complete rest from structured training

### Mesocycle (4-6 week blocks)
- **Progressive Loading**: 3 weeks building intensity/volume, 1 week deload (-40% volume)
- **Recovery Week**: Every 4th week reduce training load by 40%, focus on sleep and nutrition
- **Adaptation Window**: The body adapts during recovery, not during training - recovery weeks are as important as build weeks
- **Assessment**: Test FTP or 20-minute power at the end of each block to track progress

### Microcycle (Weekly)
- **Hard-Easy Pattern**: Schedule hard efforts followed by recovery days
- **Training Days**: 2-3 quality sessions per week maximum for amateur cyclists
- **Rest Days**: 1-2 complete rest days per week, never less than 1
- **Weekend Warrior Plan**: Saturday = long ride, Sunday = recovery spin, rest of week = 2 interval sessions

## Intervals

### VO2max Intervals (Zone 5)
- **Type A**: 5 x 3-4 min at 105-120% FTP, 3 min active recovery at Z1
- **Type B**: 6-8 x 2 min at 110-115% FTP, 1-2 min recovery at Z1
- **Type C**: 10 x 1 min all-out sprint, 2 min recovery (Tabata-inspired)
- **Weekly Volume**: 15-20 minutes total time at VO2 max effort per week
- **Best For**: Raising lactate threshold, improving sprint ability, increasing VO2 max

### Threshold Intervals (Zone 4)
- **Classic**: 2 x 8-10 min at 95-105% FTP (lactate threshold), 5 min recovery at Z1
- **Over-Under**: 2 x (2 min at 105% FTP + 1 min at 120% FTP), 5 min recovery between sets
- **Sweet Spot**: 3 x 10-15 min at 90-94% FTP (just below threshold), very time-efficient
- **Weekly Volume**: 10-15 minutes total at threshold per week for experienced riders
- **Best For**: Increasing Time to Exhaustion, improving sustainable power output

### Sprint Intervals (Zone 6)
- **Type A**: 8 x 30 seconds at max effort, 90 seconds recovery at Z1
- **Type B**: 4 x 45 seconds from 50kph rolling, full standing start
- **Type C**: 10 x 10 seconds from 30kph, neuromuscular recruitment
- **Recovery**: Full recovery between sprints - 90 seconds minimum, 2 minutes preferred
- **Best For**: Neuromuscular power, sprint technique, finishing kick

### Over-Under Intervals
- **Structure**: Alternating above and below threshold - designed to improve lactate buffering
- **Example**: 2 x (3 min at 95% FTP + 1 min at 105% FTP), 4 min recovery
- **Variation**: 3 x (2 min at 100% FTP + 1 min at 110% FTP), 3 min recovery
- **Difficulty**: High - these are mentally and physically demanding
- **Best For**: Advanced riders, race preparation, improving lactate clearance rates

### Hill Repeats (Strength-Endurance)
- **Format**: 6-10 x 2-4 min climbing effort at 90-95% FTP on gradient 5-10%
- **Cadence**: Low cadence (60-70 RPM) to build muscular strength
- **Recovery**: Descent recovery, 2-3 minutes at Z1
- **Benefit**: Builds muscular endurance, improves climbing efficiency, strengthens slow-twitch fibers
- **Progression**: Start with 4 x 2min, build to 10 x 5min over several weeks

## Weekly Structure

### Beginner Plan (8-12 hours/week)
- **Monday**: Rest or 30 min Zone 1 easy spinning
- **Tuesday**: 45 min Zone 2 endurance + 3 x 5 min threshold intervals
- **Wednesday**: 30 min Zone 1-2 recovery ride or rest
- **Thursday**: 1 hour Zone 2 base with 5 x 30 seconds sprint bursts
- **Friday**: Rest
- **Saturday**: 2-3 hour Zone 2 long ride with 1 coffee stop
- **Sunday**: 1-1.5 hour Zone 1-2 recovery ride or hike

### Intermediate Plan (10-14 hours/week)
- **Monday**: Rest or 45 min Zone 1 spinning
- **Tuesday**: 90 min Zone 2 + 4 x 8 min at threshold (FTP)
- **Wednesday**: 45 min Zone 1-2 recovery ride, focus on pedaling smoothness
- **Thursday**: 75 min Zone 2 + 6 x 3 min VO2 max intervals
- **Friday**: Rest or 30 min light spinning
- **Saturday**: 3-4 hour Zone 2 long ride, incorporate nutrition practice
- **Sunday**: 1 hour Zone 1-2 active recovery with core exercises

### Advanced Plan (14-18 hours/week)
- **Monday**: Rest or 60 min Zone 1 recovery
- **Tuesday**: 2 hour Zone 2 base + 3 x 12 min sweet spot (90-94% FTP)
- **Wednesday**: 90 min Zone 1-2 recovery + 30 min strength training (core/gym)
- **Thursday**: 90 min Zone 2 + 2 x (4 min Z5 + 4 min Z4) over-unders
- **Friday**: 60 min Zone 1-2 or complete rest before weekend
- **Saturday**: 4-5 hour Zone 2 long ride with 1 long tempo section (45 min at Z3-Z4)
- **Sunday**: 1.5 hour Group ride or Zone 2 endurance with short efforts

## Progression Rules
- **10% Weekly Rule**: Increase total training time/training stress by no more than 10% per week
- **Deload Every 4th Week**: Reduce volume by 40%, maintain some intensity at reduced duration
- **3:1 Loading Pattern**: 3 weeks progressive build, 1 week recovery/deload
- **Phase Transitions**: Gradual transition between phases - don't jump from base to peak in one week
- **Track Progression**: Record FTP, heart rate, and perceived effort every 4-6 weeks
- **When to Back Off**: If resting HR is elevated 5+ BPM above normal, take an extra rest day
- **Plateau Busting**: If progress stalls for 3+ weeks, change training stimulus - new intervals, different terrain, cycling-specific strength work

## Recovery Week Structure
- **Volume Reduction**: Cut total training volume by 40-50%
- **Keep Some Intensity**: Maintain 1-2 short quality sessions to preserve fitness
- **Extra Sleep**: Aim for 8-10 hours during recovery week
- **Massage/Self-care**: Foam rolling, stretching, massage guns - invest in recovery modalities
- **No Hard Efforts**: No intervals, no races, no long rides above Zone 2
- **Mental Reset**: Use recovery weeks to re-evaluate goals and enjoy riding without pressure
